Capacitive ECG acquisition & signal processing

Researcher · Master’s thesis · University of Porto

I designed and tested a battery-isolated capacitive ECG system, combining commercial active electrodes with floating AC coupling and capacitive driven-right-leg feedback. My work covered TINA-TI simulation, KiCad PCB design, Python signal processing and experimental validation.

Single-wrist acquisition did not yield a recognisable ECG, so I validated the prototype on a chest band. Four simultaneous recordings across three subjects achieved R-peak detection F1 of 0.97–1.00 and RR-interval bias within 2 ms of wet-electrode ECG. Waveform distortion and contact sensitivity limit the demonstrated use to rhythm and heart-rate monitoring.

Brain–heart interactions in epilepsy

Erasmus+ research internship · Denmark

I processed simultaneous EEG and ECG, extracted heartbeat-evoked potential features and compared machine-learning models to distinguish periods before and after temporal lobe seizures. The study used 33 seizures from 12 patients in the Siena Scalp EEG Dataset.

Random Forest achieved a mean ROC AUC of 0.8272 and balanced accuracy of 0.7762 with five-fold cross-validation grouped by seizure. SHAP highlighted response consistency across heartbeats. This pilot result describes separability within the dataset; patient-level generalisation and seizure prediction remain to be tested.

I am second author of Analysis of Heartbeat Evoked Potentials in Temporal Lobe Epilepsy, with Matteo Saibene and Sadasivan Puthusserypady.
