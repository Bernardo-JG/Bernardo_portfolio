Bernardo Justiça Godinho

I'm a biomedical engineer interested in understanding how things work and building tools that put that knowledge into practice.

My work brings together programming, data analysis and hands-on engineering. At DTU, I studied brain–heart interactions through physiological signals and machine learning. At INESC TEC, I developed a capacitive ECG system, working from the electronics through to signal processing and experimental testing.

I enjoy moving between research and practical projects - training models, developing applications and experimenting with hardware. Here you'll find the work I've done so far and the ideas I'm continuing to explore.
